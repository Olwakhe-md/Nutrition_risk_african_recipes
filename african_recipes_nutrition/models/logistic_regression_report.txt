=================================================================
  KEY FINDINGS
=================================================================
1. Energy is the strongest driver of HIGH risk — stronger than sodium.
   Our rule-based scorer weighted sodium #1, but the data disagrees at
   the High level. Energy (calories) is the primary trigger.

2. Sodium and fat are nearly equal for VERY HIGH risk (3.23 vs 3.16).
   The sodium-first weighting is validated at the extreme end of the scale.

3. Carbohydrate is the weakest predictor across all classes.
   Despite African cuisine being carbohydrate-heavy, carbs alone do not
   drive nutritional risk.

4. Protein has a negative coefficient for High risk (protective effect).
   Confirms the inverted protein threshold in the rule-based scorer.

5. Baseline Macro-F1 = 0.797 suggests the data is mostly linearly
   separable. The rule-based thresholds capture the right structure.
